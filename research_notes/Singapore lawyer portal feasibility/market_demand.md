# Singapore legal services market and consumer demand for finding lawyers online

Note on method: WebFetch was blocked by the egress proxy for lawsociety.org.sg, mlaw.gov.sg, singstat.gov.sg and legalbusinessonline.com, so figures below come from search-result summaries (search snippets), not full-page reads. Re-verify key numbers against primary sources before publishing.

## Supply: number of lawyers and law practices, and share of small firms

### Takeaway
Singapore has roughly 1,150 law practices and about 6,200-6,500 local practising lawyers (about 7,400-8,100 including foreign lawyers). About 80% of Singapore law practices have 1-5 lawyers, which is the likely portal customer base (roughly 900+ firms).

### Cited Findings
- 6,512 practising lawyers in 2023 (3,677 male, 2,835 female) — [Wikipedia: Lawyers in Singapore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lawyers_in_Singapore) (secondary; Law Society stats page is the primary: [Law Society statistics](https://www.lawsociety.org.sg/news-media/statistics/))
- December 2024: 8,077 lawyers registered in total, of which 6,206 Singapore solicitors at local practices and 1,374 foreign lawyers — [Wikipedia: Lawyers in Singapore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lawyers_in_Singapore) (secondary)
- LSRA data, early May 2023: 1,152 law practices and 7,429 lawyers — [Legal Business Online](https://www.legalbusinessonline.com/sg-now-home-more-1000-law-firms-6300-lawyers-report)
- About 80% of law practices are small firms with 1-5 lawyers; 174 mid-sized firms (6-30 lawyers); 23 large firms (>30 lawyers) — [Legal Business Online](https://www.legalbusinessonline.com/sg-now-home-more-1000-law-firms-6300-lawyers-report)
- Singapore Law Practices plus Foreign Law Practices account for 98% (1,134 of 1,152); about 81% of SLPs employ 1-5 lawyers, 16% employ 6-30 — [Legal Business Online](https://www.legalbusinessonline.com/sg-now-home-more-1000-law-firms-6300-lawyers-report) (as summarised by search tool)
- More than 130 foreign law firms operate in Singapore; 9 Qualifying Foreign Law Practices as of Oct 2025 — [MinLaw](https://www.mlaw.gov.sg/update-on-qualifying-foreign-law-practice-licences/)
- Public directories already exist: the LSRA "Search for a Lawyer or Law Practice" lists all practising lawyers, searchable by practice area — [MinLaw LSRA](https://eservices.mlaw.gov.sg/lsra/search-lawyer-or-law-firm/)
- Legal industry GVA was S$2.3-2.4B in 2019 with 13,000+ professionals (pre-2023, dated) — [MinLaw Tech & Innovation Roadmap](https://www.mlaw.gov.sg/files/news/press-releases/2020/10/Minlaw_Tech_and_innovation_Roadmap_Report.pdf)
- MinLaw committee report on law practice regulation, Oct 2025 (may contain updated structure data; not read) — [Report](https://www.mlaw.gov.sg/files/committeereport16oct2025.pdf)

### Inferences
- With 1,152 practices and ~80% at 1-5 lawyers, about 900 small firms (~920 estimated) are the addressable portal customer base. Mid-sized (174) are secondary targets; the 23 large firms are unlikely to need a portal.
- Roughly 6,200 local lawyers across ~1,100 local practices implies about 5-6 lawyers per firm on average, skewed by a few large firms.

### Gaps
- Sole proprietor vs partnership vs LLP vs LLC split not found.
- Firm counts for 2024-2026 not found; the latest is May 2023. Note the lawyer totals differ across sources (6,512 / 7,429 / 8,077) because of local-only vs including foreign lawyers and different dates.
- Market revenue: only a low-quality market-research estimate (USD 5.05B in 2024, [Deep Market Insights](https://deepmarketinsights.com/vista/insights/legal-services-market/singapore)); methodology unknown, treat with caution. No SingStat revenue figure retrieved.

## Demand by practice area and typical fees

### Takeaway
Family law is the largest individual-consumer segment (7,382 divorces in 2024), followed by conveyancing (about 29k HDB resales plus about 14k private resales), with high-volume low-fee wills/LPAs. Uncontested divorce, conveyancing and LPAs are low-ticket, standardisable services suited to a portal; contested litigation is higher-value.

### Cited Findings
- Divorces and annulments: 7,382 in 2024, up 3.7% from 7,118 in 2023; civil divorces 5,638 (2024) vs 5,649 (2023); Muslim divorces up 20% and about 20% of total — [SingStat Statistics on Marriages and Divorces 2024](https://assets.app.optical.gov.sg/singstat/production/published/assets/1643130e-6f04-46c1-a6a8-11953fec431f.pdf) (via search summary)
- Median marriage duration at divorce 12 years in 2024; median age at divorce men 44.4, women 40.9 — [SingStat](https://www.singstat.gov.sg/-/media/files/publications/population/smd2024.ashx)
- "Unreasonable behaviour" over 58% of civil divorce filings in 2024 — [Singapore Divorce Lawyer blog](https://www.singaporedivorcelawyer.com.sg/latest-divorce-trends-in-singapore-2025/) (law firm blog, secondary)
- Family Justice Courts 2023: 28,203 total cases handled; 22,071 filed in the "Divorce, Originating Summons & Probate & Summons" category — [Judiciary caseload statistics 2023](https://www.judiciary.gov.sg/who-we-are/statistics/caseload-statistics-2023)
- Divorce fees: uncontested S$1,500-3,500 (from about S$1.6k with no children/assets, about S$2.6k+ with children/assets); settled at mediation S$5,000-12,000; full contest S$10,000-35,000; hourly rates about S$350 (junior), S$500 (senior), S$800 (partner) — [SingSaver](https://www.singsaver.com.sg/blog/divorce-singapore-cost-contested-uncontested-divorce), [A.W. Law](https://awlaw.com.sg/legal-blog/how-much-does-a-divorce-cost-in-singapore/), [JCP Law](https://jcplaw.com.sg/practice-areas/divorce/) (firm marketing pages; indicative only)
- HDB resale transactions: 28,876 in 2024 (vs 26,735 in 2023) — [Real Estate Asia / HDB data via search](https://realestateasia.com/residential/news/singapore-hdb-resale-transactions-hit-27000-units-year)
- Private residential resale 14,053 (2024) and 14,622 (2025); sub-sales 1,428 (2024), 1,055 (2025) — [URA 4Q2025](https://www.ura.gov.sg/news/media/pr26-05/)
- Conveyancing fees: HDB resale with private lawyer S$1,500-3,000 (typically S$1,800-2,500); HDB in-house legal from about S$288 for HDB-loan buyers (under S$1,000) — [PropertyGuru](https://www.propertyguru.com.sg/property-guides/complete-guide-to-paying-for-a-resale-flat-in-singapore-9921), [YY Lee](https://www.yylee.sg/media-features-articles/conveyancing-fees-singapore)
- LPA Form 1 lawyer fee S$300-700 (+S$25-200 certification); Form 2 S$500-1,500; simple will S$200-400; will+LPA bundle about S$500; couple package about S$800 — [A.W. Law](https://awlaw.com.sg/legal-blog/what-is-a-lasting-power-of-attorney-lpa-singapore/), [Lawyer Near Me SG](https://lawyernearme.sg/will-writing-services-comparing-legal-fees-across-singapore-areas/) (indicative blogs)
- About 87,000 electronic LPAs were affected by a 2024 drafting gap (Nov 2022-Jan 2024), indicating a large volume of e-LPAs (many DIY via OPG, not lawyers) — [search summary of Mental Capacity (Amendment) Act 2024](https://www.yylee.sg/media-features-articles/register-lasting-power-of-attorney-singapore)
- Legal Aid Bureau: about 6,580 legal aid/assistance/advice cases in 2024, about 3,290 (roughly half) matrimonial; PCHI threshold S$1,050 from 1 Apr 2024 — [Legal Aid Bureau](https://lab.mlaw.gov.sg/), [MinLaw PQ 2022](https://www.mlaw.gov.sg/news/parliamentary-speeches/2022-02-14-written-answer-by-minister-for-law-k-shanmugam-to-pq-on-annual-number-of-legal-aid-applications-received-by-legal-aid-bureau/)

### Inferences
- Rough annual fee pool for individual-consumer volumes: divorce about 5,600 civil cases x S$1.5k-35k; conveyancing about 43k transactions (HDB + private resale) x S$1.5-3k = about S$65-130M if all use a lawyer (illustrative, not sourced; many HDB loan buyers use HDB's in-house legal service).
- Wills/LPAs are low-ticket (S$200-1,500) and compete with DIY/will-writing services and OPG e-LPA, so lead value is low.

### Gaps
- No Singapore-specific demand data found for criminal, personal injury, employment, immigration (e.g. State Courts caseload, MOM claims), or probate/grant counts.
- No count of wills made per year or LPAs registered per year (only the 87,000 e-LPA figure).
- 2024 Family Justice Courts writ counts not retrieved; fee ranges come from law-firm marketing pages, not independent surveys.

## How consumers find lawyers today

### Takeaway
Official channels (LSRA/MinLaw directory, Law Society directory, "Find a Featured Lawyer" paid listing, Pro Bono SG LawGoWhere, courts and LAB) exist, but no consumer study on search behaviour was found. Anecdotal evidence points to Google search, referrals and reviews dominating.

### Cited Findings
- LSRA online directory lists all practising lawyers with search by practice area — [MinLaw LSRA](https://eservices.mlaw.gov.sg/lsra/search-lawyer-or-law-firm/)
- Law Society "Find A Featured Lawyer" lists only practices that chose to advertise; not exhaustive — [Law Society (via search summary)](https://www.lawsociety.org.sg/)
- Pro Bono SG's LawGoWhere is a first-stop portal for people in legal crises — [Wikipedia: Law Society of Singapore / MinLaw resources](https://www.mlaw.gov.sg/news/announcements/legal-support-resources-available-to-members-of-the-public/)
- Forums used to be a main source of lawyer recommendations; with their decline, reviews and testimonials are the main indicator — [SingaporeLegalAdvice.com](https://singaporelegaladvice.com/law-articles/how-to-find-a-good-lawyer/) (blog)
- Free legal help: Legal Aid Bureau (means-tested), Community Legal Clinics, courts' legal help pages — [Judiciary](https://www.judiciary.gov.sg/legal-help-support/civil-case), [LAB](https://lab.mlaw.gov.sg/)

### Inferences
- A large share of discovery is via Google and referrals (property agents for conveyancing, banks, friends), but no survey quantifies this.
- The LSRA directory is a regulatory register without reviews/pricing, leaving a gap that a portal could fill (price transparency, reviews).

### Gaps
- No Singapore survey (e.g. Law Society/MinLaw public legal needs study) on channels used to find lawyers.
- No monthly Google search volume data for Singapore legal keywords (Ahrefs/Semrush unavailable); no Law Society directory traffic.

## Lawyer marketing spend and Google Ads CPC

### Takeaway
Competitive Singapore legal keywords reportedly cost S$15-30+ per click, per agency sources, so small firms face high acquisition costs. No hard data on aggregate marketing spend.

### Cited Findings
- CPCs for terms such as "divorce lawyer Singapore" and "criminal lawyer Singapore" regularly exceed SGD 15-30 per click — [marketingagency.sg: Google Ads for Lawyers](https://marketingagency.sg/google-ads-for-lawyers/) (agency marketing content, unverified, no tool screenshot)
- Long-tail keywords ("HDB conveyancing lawyer fees", "wrongful dismissal Singapore", "will writing Singapore") are cheaper and lower competition — [marketingagency.sg](https://marketingagency.sg/law-firm-marketing-singapore/)

### Inferences
- At S$20 CPC and an assumed 5-10% click-to-lead conversion, cost per lead would be about S$200-400 (assumption, unsourced), which is material against a S$1.5-3.5k uncontested divorce fee; this supports a lower-cost lead channel.

### Gaps
- No verified Ahrefs/Semrush/Google Keyword Planner data (tools unavailable; Ahrefs MCP failed to connect).
- No estimate of total law firm marketing spend in Singapore.
