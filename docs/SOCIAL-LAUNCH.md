## 2. First messages to firms

Rules: send from the Layered account or Page, not a personal one. Maximum 5 to 10 a day. Personalise the first line. Never send the same text twice in a row to the same person. If they say no or do not reply to the follow-up, stop.

### Instagram or Facebook DM, first message (short)

Hi {first name}, I'm {your name} from Layered, a Singapore directory for comparing interior designers and renovation firms. I really like {specific thing: e.g. the {project} you posted / how you present your carpentry}. Would you like a free profile on Layered? It takes about ten minutes, you use your own photos, and enquiries from homeowners go to your dashboard. Happy to set it up with you. https://layeredsg.com

### Follow-up (once, after 5 to 7 days)

Hi {first name}, a quick follow-up on my message about a free Layered profile for {company}. No pressure. If it is easy I can set it up for you from your own photos and details. Just say yes or no and I will not message again.

### If they ask "what is the catch?"

Free to list. For the first 3 months you see the full contact details of every homeowner enquiry. After that, enquiries still reach your dashboard with the project details, but the homeowner's name, phone and email are hidden unless you take a plan or unlock a single enquiry. You can remove your profile at any time.

### Email version (use if the firm lists a business email)

Subject: A free profile for {company} on Layered

Hi {first name},

I'm {your name} from Layered (layeredsg.com), a directory that helps Singapore homeowners compare interior designers and renovation firms. I liked {specific thing}.

If you would like to try it, reply and I will set up your profile with you, or you can sign up yourself at https://layeredsg.com/signup. If not, no problem and I will not write again.

(Reply "no" to be left out.)

## 3. What to collect from each firm

Ask for these in the first reply, so you can set the profile up for them (the full permission wording is in `FIRM-OUTREACH.md`):

- [ ] Company name exactly as they want it shown, and the person's name
- [ ] Login email (their own) and mobile number for lead alerts
- [ ] Type: interior design, renovation trade or landed specialist
- [ ] Property types (HDB, condo, landed, commercial) and styles
- [ ] Service areas
- [ ] Short bio (2 to 4 sentences) in their own words
- [ ] Logo file (and a "yes" that Layered may show it)
- [ ] 3 to 6 projects: title, property type, style, a short description, photos they own, and the credit line they want
- [ ] A written "yes" for each photo (WhatsApp or email is fine; save it with the date)
- [ ] HDB licence number and CaseTrust tier, if any (you verify on the official lookups before the badge shows as verified)
- [ ] Link to their Google Maps listing, if they want a "reviews on Google" button
- [ ] A contact for takedown requests

## 4. Daily routine (15 minutes)

1. Pick 5 to 10 firms from the Instagram hashtags #interiordesignsg, #hdbrenovation and #renovationsg, or from the HDB licensed-contractor directory.
2. Open each profile and note one specific thing you like.
3. Send the first message. Log it in a sheet: firm, date, channel, reply.
4. After 5 to 7 days send the one follow-up to anyone who has not replied.
5. When a firm says yes, send the intake list in section 3.
