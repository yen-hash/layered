# Inviting renovation trades and landed specialists onto Layered

Messages for recruiting the businesses in `/services` (renovation trades) and `/landed` (architects, engineers and
landed builders). For interior design firms, use `FIRM-OUTREACH.md`. The photo-permission rules there apply here too.

## Ground rules

- **Be honest that Layered is new.** Don't quote traffic, lead numbers or rankings you can't show. The pitch is a
  free, early listing in front of homeowners who are already planning a renovation.
- **Send each business its own signup link**, so the right category is already picked:
  `https://layeredsg.com/signup?category={slug}` (slugs are listed in the table below).
- **Personalise the first line.** Mention a project, review or post of theirs you actually saw. Five personal
  messages beat fifty pasted ones.
- **Identify yourself and offer an opt-out** in every message ("Reply STOP and I won't contact you again"), and honour
  it the same day. Before sending marketing messages by SMS, WhatsApp or call to a personal mobile number, check
  Singapore's Do Not Call rules. Keep a simple log of who you contacted, when and how.
- **Follow up once**, about a week later. If there's no reply, stop.

## Where to find businesses

- **Trades:** Google Maps searches by trade and estate, Carousell services listings, Facebook renovation groups, and
  suppliers your interior designer firms already work with. Ask listed ID firms who they recommend.
- **Architects:** practices with landed houses in their portfolio. Check the Board of Architects register before
  listing anyone as an architect.
- **Engineers:** check the Professional Engineers Board register.
- **Builders:** check BCA's directory of licensed builders.
