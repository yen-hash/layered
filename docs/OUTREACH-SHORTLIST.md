Compiled 2026-10-15 for inviting firms to list a free profile on Layered (layeredsg.com). Every fact below was read from the firm's own website (URLs in "Sources") by an automated page fetch/summary on that date. **Nothing has been verified against the official HDB licensed-renovation-contractor lookup or the CaseTrust lookup.** "Stated credentials" only records what the firm's own page says or shows, nothing more. No one has been contacted. Emails are copied exactly as shown on the firm's own pages; none were guessed or constructed. Where a cell says "unknown" or "none listed", I could not find it on the page.

Result: **12 firms**, not 15. I excluded candidates that were on the right track but where I could not see any sign of recent activity on their own site (undated portfolio, no dated posts), plus Carpenters, Mu Design Studio, HomeTrust, Qanvast and other directories as instructed. See "Not included" at the end.

Note on recency: most portfolios on these sites carry no per-project dates. "Recent activity" below is therefore based on dated blog posts, dated client reviews, 2026 copyright lines, or named recent BTO launches, and is stated per firm.

Red flags and fit notes (verify before any outreach):

- Earth ID: strong recency (three blog posts Jul to Sep 2026). Blog is SEO-flavoured. Project dates not shown. No public email.
- D5 Studio Image: boutique, most clearly dated work of the list. Property types on site are vague (no explicit HDB/condo labels), so HDB/condo fit is inferred from "flat" and "apartment" wording. No public email.
- The Alchemists Design: homepage hero text is placeholder copy ("Lorem Ipsum" style), so the page looks partly unfinished. Larger, multi-showroom firm. Claims "multi-award winning" without naming awards.
- Renologist: blog dates for Jan to Oct omit the year; I inferred 2026 from ordering. Multiple offices; mid-size. Project pages undated.
- MET Interior: mid-size (claims 500+ condo projects since 2011); not strictly boutique. Badges are images with no registration numbers. Blog guides undated.
- Space Atelier: mid-size (around 300 reviews on directories, two showrooms); not boutique. Blog posts show day and month only (15 Oct and earlier); year inferred. Portfolio undated.
- Urban Home Design: portfolio undated; only recency signal is the 2026 footer. Fixed prices per project shown on site; no discounting claims seen.
- Design4Space: three showrooms, mid-size. Last blog post seen was 16 May 2025, so recency rests on recent BTO project names (not dated). Check before inviting.
- Stylemyspace: newest dated article seen is 21 Feb 2025 (about 20 months old). Portfolio undated. Borderline on "active". Contact is a registered DRC per its own claim only.
- The Interior Lab: boutique-to-mid. Email protection hides the address, so it is recorded as none listed. Portfolio undated, but blog is highly active.
- iPoise: luxury positioning, may be a lower-volume fit for HDB. Project dates on page are not shown; the July 2026 date is the homepage post.
- Space Factor: calls itself "luxury"; mix of HDB and landed. No dated posts or projects; recency based on named styles and 2026 uploads only, which is weak. Check before inviting.
