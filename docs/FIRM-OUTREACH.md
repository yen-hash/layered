# Inviting real firms onto Layered

Use this when replacing the demo firms with real ones. For renovation trades and landed specialists, see `TRADE-OUTREACH.md`. Do not copy photos from a firm's website or social media:
a credit line does not give Layered the right to publish them. Ask, get a yes in writing, and keep it.

## Email 1: invitation

Subject: Your free profile on Layered (Singapore renovation directory)

Hi {first name},

I'm {your name} from Layered, a directory that helps Singapore homeowners compare interior designers and renovation
firms and read honest guides before they hire.

We'd like to list {company}. A profile is free and takes about ten minutes:

- Create an account at {site URL}/signup and fill in your profile, areas and styles.
- Add projects with your own photos. You can edit or remove them at any time.
- If you hold an HDB renovation licence or CaseTrust accreditation, add the details. We show them as "self-declared"
  until we have checked them against the official lookups, then with the date we checked.
- Homeowner enquiries that match your work go straight to your dashboard.

Layered does not rank firms or sell guarantees. Reviews only come from homeowners who enquired through Layered, and
we read every review before it is published.

Happy to answer any questions, or to set the profile up with you on a call.

{name}, {phone}, {email}

## Email 2: only if you want to use a firm's photos before they sign up

Subject: Permission to feature {company}'s project photos on Layered

Hi {first name},

We'd like to feature {project name} on {company}'s Layered profile. May we publish these photos {list or attach},
credited as "{credit line}"?

Please reply "Yes" if {company} owns the photos or has the photographer's permission, and tell us the exact credit
you'd like. We'll remove them promptly if you ask, at {takedown email}.

Thank you,
{name}

### What to keep

Save each reply (with its date) in a shared folder named by firm. In the admin tools, record the date and who
agreed in the project description until a permissions field is added. If a firm asks you to take something down,
do it the same day.

## Checklist before a firm goes live

- [ ] The firm's own account, or a signed-off reply for each photo you upload for them
- [ ] Credit line matches what they asked for
- [ ] HDB licence and CaseTrust details entered, and checked on the official lookups if you want the "Checked" mark
- [ ] A takedown contact visible to the firm
